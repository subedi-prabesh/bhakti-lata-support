Bhakti Lata – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Bhakti Lata ("the app") is a devotional app with bhajans, lyrics, a Vaishnava calendar, japa and sadhana tools. We keep what we collect to a minimum. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • Your sadhana, japa counts, favourites, playlists, notes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Location. If you tap "Use my current location" in the calendar, the app reads your location once to find the nearest city in the built-in list and to compute sunrise, tithi and Ekadashi for it. Only an approximate (city-level) location is used. It is kept on your device and is not sent to us or to any server. Location is optional: you can instead search and choose any city worldwide. Calendar reminders are scheduled on your device and are never sent to a server.
  • Photos. If you attach screenshots to feedback, the app reads the photos you choose. They are not uploaded by the app.

The random ID is generated on your device and is not linked to your name, phone number or email. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Community prayers and safety

Prayers are reviewed before they appear publicly. You can report any prayer or block an author from the prayer card. Reported content is hidden for you immediately and reviewed by us. To report or request removal of a prayer you posted, email us at the address below with the date and wording of the prayer.

Third parties

  • Google Firebase (database and notifications) processes the data listed above.
  • YouTube and other websites (for example vedabase.io and iskcon.org) open inside the app for videos and scripture. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.
  • GitHub hosts some audio files streamed to your device; GitHub can see standard request information such as your IP address.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings or the app's notification settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
  • To have your push token or a prayer you posted deleted from our servers, email us. Please include your prayer's text and date, or ask us to remove device data and we will explain how to identify it.
