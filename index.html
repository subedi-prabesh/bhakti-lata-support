Bhakti Lata: Vaishnava Bhajans and Sadhana – Support

Bhakti Lata is a devotional app with bhajans and lyrics, a Vaishnava calendar, japa counter, sadhana tracker and a virtual altar.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. The app works without signing in.

Do you need my location? Only if you tap "Use my current location" in the calendar. You can also choose any city from the list.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To remove a prayer you posted, email us.

How do I turn reminders off? Use the switch on the calendar's Today card, or your iPhone's notification settings.
